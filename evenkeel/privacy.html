Even Keel privacy policy

Even Keel is a private app used by one family. It is not offered to the public.

Google Drive

Even Keel asks Google only for access to files it creates itself (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.

It uses that access only to store its own backups, encrypted before they leave the family's computer, in the family's own Google Drive.

Sharing

Even Keel does not share, sell or send any data to anyone else, and it does not use the data for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
